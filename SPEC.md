## Judge criteria (in order)

1. Reads as a fortune cookie message for a crypto trader who was just
   liquidated, with the cookie voice and the trading loss both in it.
2. Makes a liquidated trader laugh rather than cry: the joke lands on the
   loss and on the trader, rueful and warm, never cruel and never smug.
3. One line, at most 20 words, reading whole in a single breath the way a
   real fortune does.
4. Fresh: a turn, a double meaning or a surprising last word — not the
   obvious liquidation pun anyone would reach for.
5. Keeps the fortune voice: small, oracular, slightly formal phrasing that
   the punchline then breaks.

## Why it should win

Most fortunes will reach for the obvious liquidation pun ("your margin
called — it wants its money back"). This one keeps the oracular voice
("The stars say buy the dip") and then breaks it with the last word: the
stars themselves were liquidated. The loss is in the joke, the trader
laughs with the cookie instead of being lectured, and the twist is fresh —
the oracle is a fellow victim, not a smug adviser. Eleven words, one
breath, whole.
